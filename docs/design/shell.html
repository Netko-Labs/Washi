Trash

Deleted pages wait here, with the pages inside them, until you restore them or delete them for good.

Delete all for good

▥Roadmap · Q3Deleted yesterday · with 3 pages inside↶ RestoreDelete forever
☷Weekly review · draftDeleted 3 days ago↶ RestoreSure? Delete
▤Onboarding checklistDeleted 12 days ago · with 1 page inside↶ RestoreDelete forever
1 2 3
/trash. Each row is one delete — a batch under one deleted_at stamp — so Restore and Delete forever act on the page and everything that went with it (with 3 pages inside). 1 Empty trash armed: the first click turns it into Delete all for good, the second within 3s does it, leaving the button disarms. 2 the row's icon and title are what the page had. 3 per row the same arm: Delete forever → Sure? Delete. Nothing to type: a phrase is a lock, and the stamp already makes the batch deliberate. Empty, it is the sleeping mascot and Nothing in the trash.
Restore · parent gone
  1. A restored page whose parent was purged (or is itself still in the trash) comes back at the top level of the tree — sheet-tree.ts drops the orphaned parent_id on restore.
  2. No dialog, no "where should this go?": the page is back and visible in the sidebar; Move to… is one menu away if it belongs elsewhere.
  3. Purge deletes the discussion with the page; mentions on a trashed page are hidden, not deleted, until then.
Decisions
  1. Two-click arm over typed confirm: it is reversible until the second click and fails safe on mouse-leave; typing a phrase punishes the person who meant it.
  2. The trash is a tab like any view, so it has an address and sits in the strip; it never nests under Settings.
