02 · identity

Brand。

One sheet every screen answers to. The chibi sheet, the warm orange field and the cream paper stay; what changes is that the palette, type, voice and icons are now written down once and nowhere else.

2026-10-21keep it, tighten it — washi.html §08source: Netko-Labs/brand-assets/washitokens: ui.css · paper/tokens.css

30-second version

  1. The mark is washi-chan peeking up from the bottom edge on a warm orange field. Three masters (light, dark, tinted) on a 1024 canvas; every size is downscaled from it, never redrawn, never sharpened. The 16px read is orange field, cream character, dark eyes — if a change breaks that, the change is wrong.
  2. Two palettes, kept apart. The studio chrome wears the paper palette from ui.css (orange primary, indigo for the agent); a page wears one of eight paper themes from paper/tokens.css. Chrome never borrows a page theme, a page never reaches the chrome tokens.
  3. Four faces, four jobs. Zen Kaku Gothic New for display and brand, Karla for everything you read in the UI, JetBrains Mono for keys and code, Young Serif for a page's titles. open ui.css still names Shippori Mincho as its serif; the paper says Young Serif. Young Serif wins.
  4. Washi talks like a teammate. Sentence case, short, a middle dot for a second clause (Asleep · no key). Every tool is said in words (describeTool), never a tool name or JSON. No AI-powered, no model name nobody chose, no self-hosting pitch.
  5. Nothing moves on its own but the mascot. Chrome transitions are 150–200ms ease; washi-chan is the one thing that breathes, and prefers-reduced-motion stops it.
01

The mark

The icon is the paper itself: a sheet peeking up from the bottom edge, cream halo, glossy eyes, blush, a folded sage corner for an ear, two paws on the edge. It is a character, not a logo — which is why it also walks around inside the app as washi-chan.

Three masters, all full-bleed 1024 SVGs in brand-assets/washi/source/masters/. The grain on the field and the fibre on the paper are feTurbulence; they are part of the mark, and they are why small sizes are rendered by downscaling the 1024 — the texture should fade into warmth, not turn to noise.

Everything critical sits inside the HIG safe zone, so the system mask — macOS's 824 squircle, iOS's own, the favicon's 0.225 corner — never clips the eyes. The paws are allowed to touch the edge; the face is not. decided

Washi talks the way a good teammate writes in a shared doc: short, specific, sentence case, and never about itself. The product describes what happens; the agent says what it did, in words anyone on the team would use.

Mechanics

  • Sentence case everywhere — titles, buttons, menu items, section labels (the label is uppercased by CSS, not by typing). Title Case nowhere.
  • A middle dot · joins a state and its reason: Paused · daily stop. Never a dash, never parentheses.
  • Curly quotes around a thing the person named (Add “Fix the login loop” to issues); the page's own words are never quoted back at them.
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji in product copy. The mascot carries the mood.
  • Second person, present tense. Sign in to your workspace, not Users can sign in.

Never say

  • AI-powered, smart, magic, supercharge — Washi is a teammate who writes; say what it wrote.
  • A self-hosting pitch. Washi is always self-hosted; the badge shows the host and nothing sells it. The desktop onboarding's Self-hosted sync step is the last of these. open
  • Vault, note, document, database in copy — see Names.
  • Anything about the model, the token count, the context window. The person sees dollars (Spent today) or plan usage, nothing else.
  • Error as a heading. Washi flags an issue; a page isn't here.
05

Iconography

Line icons in chrome, a closed registry on paper, the chibi sheet for anything that stands for a place. No illustration system beyond washi-chan; no icon ever carries a colour of its own.

Chrome: Tabler

@tabler/icons-react, sized by class: size-4 (16px) in rows, buttons and the approval card; size-3.5 (14px) in chips, menus and inline labels. Stroke is Tabler's default 2 — nothing in the tree overrides it. open 1.75 sits better with Karla at 13px; set it once on an IconContext, not per icon.

Paper: the sheet icon registry

A page's icon is a name from SHEET_ICONS (paper/src/registry/specs.ts), 28 of them, drawn by the registry in the sheet's ink. WSX never carries an emoji — the validator refuses it. Page chrome is the exception: the icon picker in the studio takes a registry name or an emoji, because the chrome is the studio's, not the page's (CLAUDE.md).

06

Motion

Calm. Chrome answers in 150–200ms with an ease-out and never moves unless the person did something. The mascot is the only thing that moves on its own, and it moves because the agent is doing something real.

  • Durations: --ease: 150ms ease for colour and opacity, --ease-size: 200ms ease for anything that changes size (paper tokens). Chrome uses the same two. Nothing in the product is slower than 250ms except the mascot.
  • No entrance choreography. Panels appear; rows do not slide in; lists do not stagger. A live change from another person is absorbed without a flash — the page is simply current.
  • The mascot's rig is CSS keyframes in ui.css (wbob · wscan · wdot · wscrib · wpop · wwob · wbounce · wfloat · wbub), one per agent state. Idle bobs at 3.6s; writing scribbles at 0.4s; flagged wobbles at 0.9s. The states and when each shows are in mascot.html.
  • Reduced motion is honoured in three places: .mascot-motion * and .wsx * in the app (animation: none !important), .mascot > g in the docs kit. A new animation goes under one of those scopes or it does not ship.
  • The signal dot (Working) is the one chrome element allowed to pulse — Tailwind's animate-pulse, 2s. Needs you is a steady primary dot: an ask waits, it does not nag.

08

Do / don't

Eight rules that have already been broken once somewhere, written down so they are not broken twice.

doPut the dark mark on its own gradient, #31261E → #221A14. On a black dock it still reads warm.
don'tDraw the mark on pure black, or let the field go flat black to match .dark. The glow is the brand at night.
doDownscale from the 1024 master and let the grain fade.
don'tSharpen, re-render small, or redraw a simplified 16px version. The read is already there.
doKeep the ear sage (#AEBB9A) on light, amber (#D2A360) on dark. A topic tile may change the field, not the ear.
don'tRecolour the ear to match a theme, a status, or a holiday.
doKeep the eyes inside the 824 squircle; let the paws touch the edge.
don'tPut the face behind the system mask, crop it for a tighter favicon, or centre it — it peeks.
doUse the orange field as a mark, a tile, a focus ring, a button.
don'tUse #DF8552 or --primary as a background behind text or a panel. Paper is cream; orange is a signal.
doOne accent per screen: primary for the person's action, indigo only where Washi acted.
don'tMix a page theme's accent into the chrome, or put two coloured buttons in one view.
doLet the mascot carry the mood — a state, a face, a bob.
don'tAdd emoji, exclamation marks or a second illustration style to say the same thing.
doSay what Washi did: Updated the page — Backlog.
don'tShow sheet_replace, a JSON blob, or a model name to a person.
