03 · character

Washi-chan

The chibi sheet. A piece of washi paper peeking up from the bottom edge: cream body, glossy eyes, blush, a folded sage corner for an ear, two paws. The agent in the product is called Washi; she is its face.

2026-10-21two rigs · 240×266 and 10247 faces · 6 motions · 8 statespackages/washi/ui/src/mascot

30-second version

  1. Two rigs, one character. The small rig (240×266) is seven swappable faces on one body, <use>d from MascotDefs. The 1024 rig is the peeking body on an orange squircle, drawn whole per state; the app icon and the workspace tiles are the same drawing.
  2. Geometry is design-exact and closed. New expressions are new <g id="f…"> entries in mascot-defs.tsx, never a restyle.
  3. She is a status indicator before she is decoration. The eight agent states are computed from the transcript (mascotStateFor), and the FAB, the panel header and the page header all show the same one.
  4. Calm by construction: one motion per instance, the slowest loop for idle, nothing under 0.9s except the pencil, and every rig freezes to a still face under prefers-reduced-motion.
  5. She never speaks. Words come from the panel, the pill label and the approval card; she only shows what Washi is doing.
01

Who she is

A sheet of paper that noticed you. Curious, unhurried, never alarmed. Performed alarm is not information; a quiet face with a flag above it is.

Made of

Washi paper: cream #F7EDD6 with an ink outline, one corner folded over in sage, so she is literally the sheet the product is named for. The 1024 drawing adds a cream halo behind the body so she reads as a cut-out on the orange field at 16px (brand-assets/washi/README.md, "the read at 16px").

Temperament

Calm, curious, a little pleased with herself. Her range tops out at suggesting's open smile and bottoms out at resting's shut eyes. flagged raises the brows and floats a triangle; it does not shake, redden or widen the eyes. sad and dizzy exist for pages, not for the agent.

For

  • The product's warmth: empty states, onboarding, the sign-in pane, a <Mascot> on a page.
  • Its status indicator: the FAB, the panel header and the header pill all wear mascotStateFor(transcript, running, approvals).
  • The door to the agent: the FAB is her, and ⌘J opens the panel she sits on.

Not

  • Not a chatbot avatar. She is never beside a message bubble in the transcript (transcript-view.tsx renders text only; she appears there once, asleep, when it is empty).
  • Never talks. No speech bubbles, no thought-cloud copy. The panel speaks; the pill says Washi flagged an issue in words next to her, not from her.
  • Not an apology. A failed turn gets flagged and a receipt line, never a sad face.

05

Agent states

Eight states, one SVG each, pure CSS motion. Every surface that shows the agent shows the same state, because every surface calls the same function.

StateTrigger — mascotStateFor in stores/agent/agent-store.tsStatus
suggestingapprovals.length > 0. Checked first: a question waiting beats everything, running or not. (host.activity() agrees: needs_you wins over working.)have
idleNot running and the last transcript entry is not a turn_done — a fresh session, or a cancelled one.have
doneNot running, last entry turn_done with any stopReason but error. Holds until the next entry. open the brief says 20s then idle; the code has no timer. The 20s that exists is presence on the page (AGENT_PRESENCE_TTL_MS), not this.have
flaggedNot running, last turn_done has stopReason === 'error'. A failed write inside a turn that then ends cleanly shows done. open whether a failed sheet_replace should flag on its own.have
readingRunning, and the latest tool_call since the last user_message has toolKind read, search or fetch.have
writingRunning, latest tool call is edit (CHANGE_TOOLS: page and table writes).have
thinkingRunning with no tool call yet in this turn (the model is streaming), or the latest tool is delete, execute, think or other.have
restingNever computed. mascotStateFor's return type excludes it; it is placed by hand on first-run (no key). The sidebar's asleep (agents.inference has no active credential) and paused (daily stop) are dots today, not her. open fold both into resting so the FAB sleeps when nothing can think.open

Scheduled routines run as ordinary turns in a fresh session that is not the active one, so the FAB does not animate for them — onAgentUpdate drops entries whose sessionId is not activeSessionId. The sidebar row's working (polled from host.activity(), any turn in flight) is the only place a routine shows. decided that stays: a routine is Washi's own business until it asks.

07

Timing and stillness

She earns attention by not asking for it. Slow loops, one at a time, and a complete stop the moment the person asks for stillness or is looking at something else.

  • One motion at a time. Mascot has a single motion prop; AgentMascot composes one body wrap with one face detail (blink, scan, scribble) and one backdrop. Never two body motions on one instance; never a bobbing mascot inside a bouncing container. decided
  • Idle breathes slowly. idle is wbob 3.6s; resting is wfloat 5s. Those two are what the FAB shows almost all day, so they are the slowest loops in the set.
  • Nothing loops faster than 0.9s except writing's pencil (wscrib 0.4s), and the pencil is a 36px prop on a still body. flagged's wwob 0.9s is the floor. decided
  • Reduced motion freezes everything. ui.css: @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) { .mascot-motion * { animation: none !important } }. AgentMascot always carries mascot-motion; Mascot only when the caller passes it. open the five Mascots in OnboardingArt.tsx and its sparkles, sheets and confetti do not, so the desktop onboarding still moves under reduced motion. Fix: put the class on Mascot's own <svg> and on the art's wrapper.
  • Never animate while a dialog is open. open nothing enforces it today; the FAB keeps bobbing under the command palette and the create-workspace dialog. Proposed: the shell sets data-dialog-open on <body> and ui.css adds [data-dialog-open] .mascot-motion * { animation-play-state: paused } — paused, not none, so she resumes where she was.
  • 20 seconds of presence. After each finished Washi write, runtime/agent-watch.ts marks her present on that page for AGENT_PRESENCE_TTL_MS = 20_000; the page's presence row shows her for that long and then she is gone, without a fade.

08

Do / don't

The character is closed. Use her more, draw her never.

doReference the face: <use href="#fCurious"/>. Add an expression by adding a <g id="f…"> to mascot-defs.tsx, a name to MascotFace, and the paper enum if pages may use it.
don'tRestyle the geometry — not a radius, not a stroke, not the blush. "design-exact — do not restyle" is the first line of the file for a reason; the icon, the tiles and the FAB all have to stay the same animal.
doLet the state carry the news. flagged with a receipt line under it says everything a failed turn needs to say.
don'tUse her to apologise. No sad face for a server error, no dizzy face for a timeout, no "oops" copy next to her. She reports; the panel explains.
doPick one motion and the slowest one that fits. breathe for anything that stays, bob for an invitation, none on a page.
don'tStack motions: no bob inside a bouncing container, no second keyframe on the wrap, no CSS transition on her position while she animates.
doShow flagged for Washi's own failures: a turn that ended in error, a write the validator refused.
don'tShow flagged for the person's error — a bad invite link, a wrong password, a WSX draft with a typo. That is the form's message, and she stays idle.
doPut words beside her: the pill label, "Washi is working…", "Washi wants to …" on the card.
don'tSpeak through her. No speech bubbles, no first-person copy anchored to the mascot, no eyes that follow the cursor. The panel speaks.
09

Implementation map

Everything is in packages/washi/ui/src/mascot plus one block of keyframes. Adding a state touches five files, in order.

defs
mascot/mascot-defs.tsx — MascotDefs(): the 0×0 SVG with #wBody and the seven #f… groups. Mounted once: apps/studio/src/components/shell/workspace-shell.tsx, apps/desktop/src/App.tsx. The docs' copy is docs/_kit/mascot.js.
small rig
mascot/mascot.tsx — Mascot({ face = 'happy', motion = 'none', size = 32, className, opacity }). FACE_IDS and MOTION_STYLES are the two tables.
agent rig
mascot/agent-mascot.tsx — AgentMascot({ state, size = 22, className, crop = false }); Body, RoundEyes({ up, blink }), HappyShut, OpenSmile, stateContent(state) → { wrap, inner, backdrop }. One clipPath per state id.
tiles
mascot/vault-icon.tsx — VaultIcon({ topic, size = 40, className }) over lib/vault-topics.tsx's VAULT_TOPICS (bg, ear, backdrop, face, extra) and VAULT_TOPIC_ORDER.
types
mascot/lib/types.ts — AgentMascotState, MascotFace, MascotMotion.
state
stores/agent/agent-store.ts — mascotStateFor(transcript, running, approvals); header/lib/hooks/use-agent-mood.ts wraps it; header/lib/values.ts holds STATE_LABELS.
keyframes
css/ui.css lines ~98–400: CamelCase for the small rig and specks, lowercase for the agent rig, then the reduced-motion rule on .mascot-motion.
page primitive
packages/shared/paper/src/registry/specs.ts (kit.Mascot, unique) and components/kit.tsx (MASCOT_FACES, MASCOT_SIZES). .wsx-mascot in css/flow-kit.css.
brand
Netko-Labs/brand-assets/washi — icon masters, tokens, washi:sync. The synced files in this repo are never edited.

Adding an agent state

  1. Draw it in agent-mascot.tsx: a new case in stateContent built from Body, RoundEyes, HappyShut, OpenSmile and props. New geometry only for a prop; the body and eyes are fixed.
  2. Add the name to AgentMascotState in lib/types.ts. The switch is exhaustive; the typecheck tells you what you missed.
  3. If it needs a motion the rig lacks, add a lowercase keyframe to ui.css next to wbub. Reuse first: ten keyframes cover eight states.
  4. Decide who produces it: a branch in mascotStateFor (and a word in STATE_LABELS) if it is a live state, or a hand placement if it is a still.
  5. Add it to this document: the grid in §05, the trigger table, the still-face row in §07.

Adding a face

  1. <g id="fName"> in mascot-defs.tsx, built from the same eye, blush and mouth vocabulary at the same coordinates.
  2. MascotFace in lib/types.ts, FACE_IDS in mascot.tsx.
  3. If pages may use it: the mood enum in specs.ts and MASCOT_FACES in kit.tsx. Both or neither.
  4. Mirror it in docs/_kit/mascot.js (DEFS and FACES) so the docs can show it, and add it to §03.
